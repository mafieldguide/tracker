# Submission policy

## What to submit

New questions, corrections, and evidence for an unofficial community FAQ about Math Academy. Include an original source URL whenever one exists. A screenshot is backup evidence, not a replacement for the link.

## How submissions are handled

Submissions are not treated as verified because someone supplied a link or screenshot. The maintainer reviews each one, checks the source independently, and writes any resulting FAQ change. Accepted issues are closed with a link to the published page; others are labelled `declined` or `duplicate`.

## Screenshots and privacy

- Crop to the smallest useful context and remove unrelated messages.
- Redact ordinary community usernames and avatars when identity isn't relevant.
- Math Academy staff identity may be kept when it matters for establishing a source's authority.
- Don't post unnecessary personal information.
- Screenshots and quotes remain the material of their respective rights holders.

## What will be removed or declined

- Content unrelated to documenting Math Academy questions
- Harassment or personal attacks
- Speculation about individuals or unsupported accusations
- Irrelevant private or personal information
- Broad screenshot dumps containing unrelated conversations
- Spam
- Account, billing, or support requests that need Math Academy itself

## Takedown and privacy requests

To request removal of unnecessary personal information or unnecessary third-party material, open an issue (or say so in an existing one). Reasonable requests are reviewed promptly. Where possible the material is removed or redacted while the factual citation record is kept through the original URL or a narrower piece of evidence. Takedown handling is not used to rewrite the historical record.

## Contact Math Academy directly for

Billing, login/account access, privacy or security incidents, account-specific bugs, and anything involving personal data or account state.
